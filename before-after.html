The phone version of your portfolio doesn't match desktop

I opened preiyalbhalla.com on desktop, tablet and phone. Desktop is the stronger version. On phones, two project cards show the wrong company, and a few details differ between sizes. The fixed page keeps your visual design (colors, type, cards, hero) and resolves each one. The Airtel title breaks mid-word on phones

The image column stays beside the text at phone width, so the title gets a narrow column and splits into "Reward / s & / engage / ment". Stacking the image under the text on narrow screens fixes it.

The email button works differently on phone and desktop

On desktop, Email copies the address and shows "Copied!", so a visitor still has to open their mail app and paste it. On phones, the menu's Email is already a real mailto: link (with a stray space after the colon). The fixed page does both on every screen: clicking opens the mail app and also copies the address, for people who use webmail.
